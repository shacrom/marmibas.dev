## The problem

Renovation companies write quotes in Word, calculate in Excel and store every document somewhere different. One badly dragged cell shifts a total without anyone noticing, and finding an invoice from six months ago is a treasure hunt. When the client receives a PDF with a mistake, they don't think "they slipped up" — they think "this company isn't serious".

## The solution

[Voxye](https://voxye.es) brings all the paperwork into one place: quotes, contracts, invoices, supplier orders and client records. You enter the line items and the application calculates the totals, generates the PDF with your logo and colours, and emails it to the client. Any employee produces the same polished document, without opening Word or touching a formula.
