## Context

Renovation companies in Spain are, for the most part, small businesses: self-employed contractors with a crew of workers, family-run firms, neighbourhood builders. Their day is split between the job site and the office: visiting clients to take measurements, drafting the quote in Word, computing line items in Excel, printing the contract, filing the invoice in a folder, calling the supplier to order materials and, in between, replying to WhatsApp messages from clients asking how the renovation is going. All the paperwork revolves around a handful of tools that were never designed for this.

## The problem

The first headache is the quotes themselves. A renovation is built from line items: labour, materials, chapters per room, discounts, VAT. Computing all of that in Excel works until someone drags a cell wrong and the total drifts by 800 euros without anyone noticing. When the client receives that PDF with the wrong number or a sloppy layout, they don't think "they made a mistake" — they think "this company isn't serious". The brand image is damaged before price is even on the table.

The second pain is fragmentation. Every document lives somewhere different: the quote in Word, the invoices in Excel, signed contracts scanned into another folder, supplier orders in an Outlook email no one knows how to search, and client conversations on WhatsApp. When a client calls six months later asking about a warranty or an invoice, finding the information turns into a treasure hunt. And often, it simply never turns up.

The third pain is inconsistency. Each employee writes quotes their own way: one uses an old template, another copies a previous job and edits the details, another starts from scratch. The result is misaligned logos, fonts that change mid-page, last year's tax data, legal terms that no longer apply. The company comes across as less professional without realising it.

## The solution

Voxye is an application that brings all the paperwork of a renovation company into one place: quotes, contracts, invoices, supplier orders, client records, products, subcontractors and on-site incidents. Any employee can generate a professional PDF with the company's branding in a couple of minutes, without opening Word or touching a single Excel formula.

It's deliberately simple to use: you enter the client's details and the line items of the renovation, and the application takes care of the rest. It calculates totals, VAT and discounts without errors, generates the PDF with your logo and your colours, and emails it to the client automatically. What used to require opening three different programs, copying data by hand and praying nothing went wrong now happens on a single screen.

## How it works day to day

**Quotes without errors.** The first headache disappears. You enter the line items, quantities and unit prices, and the application calculates totals, VAT and discounts for you. No broken Excel sheets, no miscounted totals, no quotes sent with mistakes you have to apologise for over the phone afterwards.

**Your brand on every PDF.** You upload your logo, tax data and brand colours once. Every PDF — quotes, contracts, invoices, supplier orders — comes out with the same visual identity. Professional, consistent, recognisable. The client always sees the same company, not eight different versions depending on who prepared the document.

**Everything connected.** A client accepts a quote and, with one click, it becomes a contract. The contract is signed and the invoice is generated. The invoice is paid and everything is logged in the client's history. No jumping between Word, Excel and folders. No losing track of what's been paid and what hasn't.

If you're starting your own renovation company, you don't need to invent Word templates or build spreadsheets from scratch. Voxye gives you the structure from your very first quote and grows with you as more clients and more employees come on board.

## Results

Companies that use Voxye stop wasting time on admin. Quotes reach the client faster, with a better image, and without errors that have to be corrected later. Invoices are issued in the same format every time and information is stored safely, so nothing falls through the cracks.

And because everything is kept in one place, finding a quote from six months ago takes two seconds instead of half an hour digging through folders. The company presents itself better to clients and, internally, the team works with less stress.

## Next steps

A client-facing view is on the roadmap: your clients will be able to log in and follow the progress of their renovation without having to call you for updates. Fewer phone calls, more trust, and a single channel where the information already lives.
